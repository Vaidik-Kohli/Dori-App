Architecture, Technologies & Innovation

How Dori combines ambient acoustic AI, geospatial reverse-matching, and hardware-less zero-trust security into a sub-5-second crisis resolution loop.

01 / Architecture 4x

4-Tier Crisis Resolution Pipeline

1. Edge Trigger Layer < 0.2s

1-Touch tactile button + Voice Activity Detection (VAD) opens live ambient mic.

2. AI Triage Engine 1.8s latency

Whisper Indic STT + LLM classifies severity, extracts Hindi medical intent & skills.

3. Geospatial Matching H3 Indexing

Dispatches verified Paramedic vs. companion within 3km radius matching clinical need.

4-digit ephemeral OTP on senior's screen verified by responder on doorstep before entry.

02 / Stack Tech

Core Technologies & Justification

Indic Speech AI (Whisper + LLaMA)

Why fit: Elderly demographic cannot type. Understands Hindi code-mixing and extracts clinical metadata automatically.

Uber H3 Spatial Clustering & Routing

Why fit: Sub-second spatial lookup across neighborhood hexagonal zones ensures responders arrive within <15 mins.

Ephemeral Cryptographic OTP Handshake

Why fit: Seniors living alone fear stranger entry. Generates zero-trust verification without costly smart-lock hardware.

Dual-Channel WebSockets & WebRTC

Why fit: Real-time cross-border telemetry links family in the US/UK with live GPS and audio channels directly to the responder.

Infrastructure Cloud-Native Edge Event Hub
03 / Innovation USP

Key Innovations & Differentials

1. Ambient Acoustic Triage

Bypasses all app navigation. Converting natural panic audio into structured triage data in Hindi without requiring the user to tap or articulate a formal menu.

2. Reverse Skill-Matching Engine

Traditional gig apps send anyone. Dori matches clinical skill: Paramedic for chest pain vs. tech-savvy helper for hardware failure.

3. Hardware-Less Zero-Trust Security

Eliminates the fear of impersonators. A one-time security handshake required on the doorstep before the task can be marked active.

4. Cross-Border Transparency

Instant SMS and live tracker for overseas children, turning helpless distance into real-time visibility and direct voice bridge.
