# Brag Plan: Dori (डोरी)

## What is this app?
Dori is an ambient voice AI and reverse-dispatch crisis coordination platform for elderly care in India, turning raw distress into verified on-site paramedic support in under 5 seconds.

## The angle
"Zero cognitive load for parents — from panic audio to a verified paramedic on your doorstep in under 5 seconds." While typical emergency apps demand typing and menu navigation during a crisis, Dori operates via a single tactile press and ambient acoustic listening, bridging the gap between elderly parents in Delhi and children 10,000 miles away.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast dark frame with an emerald pulse: "What happens when an elderly parent needs help?" The massive emerald tactile SOS button drops in on beat 1.60s with a satisfying click, radiating soundwaves.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Ambient Voice Triage (3.7s - 8.9s):** The button activates "Riya is calling you..." with an animated gold waveform. The AI Triage Copilot transcribes native Hindi speech ("छाती में बहुत दर्द हो रहा है") and automatically tags it as an acute Medical Emergency requiring a Paramedic.
- **Algorithmic Dispatch & Zero-Trust Verification (8.9s - 14.7s):** Sunil Verma (Verified Paramedic) accepts the dispatch. The parent's screen displays a 4-digit security code `[ 4 ] [ 0 ] [ 9 ] [ 2 ]`. On arrival, the helper inputs the code, triggering an immediate green verified lock.
- **Cross-Border Peace of Mind (14.7s - 17.9s):** The Family Hub timeline advances in real-time with live GPS telemetry, providing instant clarity to family members overseas.

## Outro / punchline
17.9s - 20.0s: Beat drops into the bold typography reveal: **Dori (डोरी)** — "Zero Cognitive Load • Algorithmic Care. Built for Bharat Innovation Challenge 2.0."

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Senior taps the tactile SOS button.
2. AI Action: Ambient mic detects distress, LLM extracts Paramedic skill and location.
3. Result: Sunil Verma dispatched, verified via on-screen OTP `4092`, and tracked live by remote family.
